About

I'm a student based in Pakistan with an interest in both technology and creative work. I spend a lot of my time learning Python and web development, exploring AI and machine learning, and building small real projects — like Discord bots I actually use and maintain.

Alongside that, I design graphics in Canva and put together polished documents in Microsoft Word, from study notes to reports and invoices. I like that development and design pull from different parts of my brain, and I try to bring the same care to both.

I'm not a professional developer or an experienced designer — I'm a student who likes building things, and this portfolio is where I keep track of what I make as I learn.
